Privacy note

How we use your enquiry details

Shree Radhe Steel Industries collects only the information you choose to send us through this website, by phone, on WhatsApp or by email.

What we collect

  • Contact details you enter: name, company, phone, WhatsApp number, email, city and state.
  • Requirement details: product, grade, form, size, quantity, delivery location, required date, your message, your enquiry list and any drawing you upload.

Why we use it

Only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quotation and arrange supply if you place an order. We do not sell your details or share them for marketing.

Where it goes

  • Enquiries sent with the quotation form are received by our website host’s form service (Netlify) and forwarded to our business email.
  • If you choose “Send on WhatsApp” or “Send by email”, the message is prepared on your device and sent through WhatsApp or your own email app.
  • Your enquiry list is saved only in your own browser on this device, so it is still there when you come back. Clearing the list, or your browser data, removes it.

Cookies and third parties

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts load from Google Fonts and code libraries from jsDelivr and cdnjs. Like any web request, those services see your IP address.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email shreeradhe.steel.alloys@gmail.com or call +91 99111 94545.
