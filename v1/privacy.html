Privacy note

How we use your enquiry details

Shree Radhe Steel Industries collects only the information you choose to send us through this website, by phone, WhatsApp or email.

What we collect

  • Contact details you enter: name, company, phone, WhatsApp number, email, city and state.
  • Requirement details: product, grade, size, quantity, delivery location, required date, message and any drawing you upload.

Why we use it

Only to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quotation, and arrange supply if you place an order. We do not sell or share your details for marketing.

Where it is stored

Website enquiries are delivered through our website host's form service and to our business email. WhatsApp messages are handled within WhatsApp.
